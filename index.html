      Settings

      Positive = notes arrive later. If you hit late, lower it; if you hit early, raise it.

      How to Play

      Notes slide down four lanes toward the white line at the bottom. Act on them the moment they touch the line — the dashed path shows where the next notes go.

      Tap

      Click, touch or press the lane key as the note reaches the line.

      Hold

      Press when the head arrives and keep holding until the tail passes the line.

      Drag

      Press the round head, then keep the button down and slide left and right along the zigzag ribbon.

      Controls

      • Mouse / trackpad: click, hold the button, drag.
      • Touchscreen: tap, hold your finger, slide.
      • Keyboard (optional): D F J K are lanes 1–4. Hold keys for holds; for drags, hold the key of the lane the ribbon is in and roll to the next key as it moves.
      • Esc / P / Space pause, R restart. Menus: Tab, arrow keys and Enter.

      Scoring

      • Perfect, Great, Good depend on how close your timing is. A miss breaks your combo and drains health.
      • Hold and drag notes also score along their length — stay on the path.
      • Health at zero ends the song unless Practice mode is on. Grades: S ≥ 95%, A ≥ 90%, B ≥ 80%, C ≥ 70%, otherwise D.
      • Same song, difficulty and seed = the same chart. Change the seed for a fresh one.
      • The game pauses if you switch tabs. If notes feel early or late, adjust Timing offset in Settings.

      Credits

      ZIGZAG — a file-based rhythm game made with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

      All visuals are drawn with Canvas, CSS and SVG, and all sound effects are synthesized in the browser. No outside assets or libraries are used.

      Songs are the ones you add to the songs folder. Demo tracks included here are generated tones made for this project.
