      Settings

      Positive = notes arrive later. If you hit late, lower it; if you hit early, raise it.

      How to Play

      Walk through Zig Zag Town to the glowing portal, or choose a stage from Stage Select. Notes slide down four lanes toward the white line. Hit each note when it reaches the line to build your score and combo.

      Tap

      Click, touch or press the lane key as the note reaches the line.

      Hold

      Press when the head arrives and keep holding until the tail passes the line.

      Drag

      Press the round head, then keep the button down and slide left and right along the zigzag ribbon.

      Controls

      • Mouse / trackpad: click, hold the button, drag.
      • Touchscreen: tap, hold your finger, slide.
      • Keyboard: D F J K are lanes 1–4. Hold keys for long notes; follow the gold ribbon across lanes for drags.
      • Walk with WASD or the arrow keys. Down switches worlds. Press Enter beside a person, gate, or portal. Use Page Up / Page Down to choose the stage.
      • In Zag, enter a Warden gate to start a boss fight. Choose ZIGZAG, then hold the mouse and trace all five lights for a powerful strike. A win opens the exit gate; press Enter or click to leave.
      • Esc / P pauses. R returns to the hub after a stage; enter the portal there to play. Stage Select is available from the main menu.

      Scoring

      • Perfect, Great, Good depend on how close your timing is. A miss breaks your combo and drains health.
      • Hold and drag notes also score along their length — stay on the path.
      • Health at zero ends the song unless Practice mode is on. Grades: S ≥ 95%, A ≥ 90%, B ≥ 80%, C ≥ 70%, otherwise D.
      • Clear a stage to unlock the next one. Stage clears and best scores are saved on this device.
      • The game pauses if you switch tabs. If notes feel early or late, adjust Timing offset in Settings.

      Credits
